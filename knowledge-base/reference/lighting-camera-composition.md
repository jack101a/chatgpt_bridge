# Lighting, Camera, and Composition Reference

Universal quality multipliers — these transfer across all platforms. This is
standard photography and cinematography vocabulary that improves any prompt,
SFW or otherwise.

## Lighting

### Natural Light
- **Golden hour** — warm, soft, long shadows. First/last hour of sunlight.
  Flattering for skin, romantic mood.
- **Blue hour** — cool, moody, twilight. Just before sunrise / after sunset.
- **Overcast** — soft, even, no harsh shadows. Natural softbox. Good for
  portraits, reduces blemishes.
- **Window light** — soft, directional. Classic portrait light. North-facing
  for consistent soft light.
- **Open shade** — even, soft, slightly cool. Subject in shadow, bright
  background.

### Studio Lighting
- **3-point lighting** — key + fill + rim/backlight. Standard interview setup.
- **Rembrandt** — key light at 45° creating triangle of light on shadowed cheek.
  Dramatic, classic.
- **Butterfly / Paramount** — key light directly above, butterfly shadow under
  nose. Glamour, 1940s.
- **Split lighting** — one side lit, one side dark. Dramatic, moody, secretive.
- **Clamshell** — key above, fill below. Beauty/glamour, smooth skin.

### Dramatic / Artistic
- **Rim lighting** — backlight creating silhouette edge. Separates subject
  from background.
- **Volumetric** — god rays, visible light beams through fog/dust. Atmospheric.
- **Chiaroscuro** — strong contrast between light and dark. Renaissance
  painting style (Caravaggio, Rembrandt).
- **Noir** — hard shadows, high contrast, venetian blind patterns. 1940s
  detective films.
- **Low-key** — predominantly dark, minimal light. Moody, intimate.
- **High-key** — bright, even, minimal shadows. Cheerful, clean, commercial.

### Light Quality
- **Soft** — diffused, flattering, wraps around subject. Cloudy day, softbox.
- **Hard** — sharp shadows, dramatic. Direct sun, bare bulb.
- **Warm** — golden/amber tones. Sunset, tungsten, cozy.
- **Cool** — blue/cyan tones. Shade, overcast, moonlight.
- **Mixed color** — warm key + cool fill (teal & orange). Cinematic.

## Camera Angles

| Angle | Effect | Use |
|-------|--------|-----|
| Eye-level | Neutral, natural | Standard portraits |
| Low angle | Powerful, heroic, imposing | Dramatic subjects |
| High angle | Vulnerable, small, isolated | Emotional vulnerability |
| Dutch angle | Unease, tension, dynamic | Action, psychological |
| Worm's-eye | Looking up, grand | Architecture, towering figures |
| Bird's-eye | Overhead, omniscient | Environment, patterns |
| POV | First-person immersion | Immersive, intimate |
| Over-shoulder | Voyeuristic, conversational | Dialogue, observation |

## Shot Sizes

| Size | Framing | Purpose |
|------|---------|---------|
| Extreme close-up | Single detail (eye, lips) | Intimacy, texture |
| Close-up | Head/face | Emotion, expression |
| Medium close-up | Head + shoulders | Portrait standard |
| Bust shot | Chest up | Fashion, upper body |
| Medium | Waist up | Interaction, gesture |
| Cowboy | Thigh up | Action, stance |
| Full body | Head to toe | Pose, figure, fashion |
| Wide | Subject + environment | Context, setting |
| Extreme wide | Landscape dominant | Establishing, scale |

## Lenses

| Focal Length | Character | Use |
|--------------|-----------|-----|
| 24mm | Wide, environmental, slight distortion | Landscapes, architecture |
| 35mm | Natural documentary | Street, environmental portrait |
| 50mm | Natural eye perspective | General purpose, natural portrait |
| 85mm | Flattering compression, bokeh | Portrait, beauty |
| 135mm | Strong compression, isolation | Tight portrait, telephoto |
| Macro | Extreme detail | Texture, detail shots |

**Effects:**
- **Wide** = more environment + slight distortion at edges
- **Telephoto** = subject isolation + background compression + bokeh
- **Shallow DOF** (f/1.4-2.8) = bokeh, subject isolation, dreamy
- **Deep DOF** (f/8-16) = everything sharp, landscape

## Composition

### Framing Techniques
- **Rule of thirds** — subject at intersection of thirds. Balanced, dynamic.
- **Leading lines** — lines drawing eye to subject. Roads, fences, rivers.
- **Foreground framing** — frame subject with foreground elements. Branchs,
  archways, windows.
- **Negative space** — empty area around subject. Minimalist, isolating.
- **Depth layering** — foreground / midground / background. Three-dimensional.
- **Symmetry** — mirrored composition. Formal, calm, powerful.
- **Golden ratio** — spiral composition. Organic, pleasing.
- **Frame within frame** — subject framed by element (doorway, mirror).

### Depth of Field
- **Shallow** (f/1.4-2.8) — bokeh, subject isolated, dreamy, intimate
- **Deep** (f/8-16) — everything sharp, landscape, environmental
- **Rack focus** — shift focus between subjects. Cinematic.

## Example Prompts Integrating All Elements

### Portrait (Flux natural language)
```
A photorealistic portrait of a woman with curly hair, medium close-up framing,
shot with an 85mm lens at f/1.8 for shallow depth of field. Soft window light
from camera left, Rembrandt lighting pattern. Rule of thirds composition with
subject on right third. Warm golden hour tones. Intimate, contemplative mood.
Highly detailed skin texture, 8k quality.
```

### Full Body (SD comma tags)
```
1girl, solo, full body, standing in field, golden hour backlight, rim lighting,
50mm lens, shallow depth of field, bokeh, rule of thirds, leading lines from

```

```
cinematic film still, woman walking rain-soaked street, low angle, 35mm
anamorphic, teal and orange color grade, volumetric light from streetlamps,
noir shadows, leading lines of wet pavement, --ar 21:9 --stylize 500 --raw
```

## Quick Reference

- **Lighting** sets mood: soft = flattering, hard = dramatic, warm = cozy,
  cool = moody.
- **Angle** sets power: low = powerful, high = vulnerable, eye = neutral.
- **Shot size** sets intimacy: close = intimate, wide = environmental.
- **Lens** sets compression: wide = environment, telephoto = isolation.
- **Composition** guides eye: thirds = balanced, leading lines = directed,
  negative space = minimalist.
- **DOF** sets focus: shallow = dreamy isolation, deep = sharp environment.
